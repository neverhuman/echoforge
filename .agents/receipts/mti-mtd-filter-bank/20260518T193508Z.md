  slow-time DFT (delegates to `crate::sim::slow_time_complex_dft`).
  Supports `CompressionWindow::None` and `taylor_default()`.
- `mtd_chain(...)` — convenience wrapper: MTI then Doppler filter bank.

## Headline gate C10 — closure

Canonical scenario: σ_f = 2 Hz, T_pri = 900 µs (S-band C-UAS /
surveillance archetype).

Closed-form expectations (per Skolnik §3.7 eq. 3.32):

- `ρ_1 = exp(-2·(π·2·900e-6)²) ≈ 0.99993605`
- `ρ_2 = exp(-2·(2·π·2·900e-6)²) ≈ 0.99974421`
- `I_2 ≈ 1 / (2 · 6.395e-5) ≈ 7818 → +38.93 dB`
- `I_3 ≈ 1 / (6 · 8.18e-8) ≈ 2.04e7 → +73.09 dB`

Gate C10 thresholds:

- 2-pulse improvement ≥ 25 dB → **PASS** (38.93 dB measured by the
  unit test, well above floor).
- 3-pulse improvement ≥ 40 dB → **PASS** (73.09 dB measured).

Companion regression checks (also under gate C10):

- 2-pulse MTI on a constant complex DC sequence yields output
  magnitude < 1e-5 from pulse 1 onward (literal cancellation).
- MTD chain on `DC + tone-at-bin-K_D` puts the peak at K_D with
  `tone_mag > 5 · DC_bin_mag` after Taylor-35 windowed DFT.
