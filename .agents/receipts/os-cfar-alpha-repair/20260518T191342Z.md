## Why

Audit finding from the EchoForge Radar Expert Credibility Sweep (Wave 1):
`crates/echoforge-radar/src/detectors/os_cfar.rs` (lines 57–67 pre-edit) was
silently delegating to `crate::cfar::ca_cfar_scale(2 * training_cells, pfa)`
to compute the OS-CFAR threshold multiplier. That formula
`alpha_CA = N * (Pfa^(-1/N) - 1)` is the Skolnik §7.7.2 closed form for
**Cell-Averaging CFAR on exponentially-distributed power** (Gaussian I+Q /
Rayleigh amplitude). Applying it to Ordered-Statistic CFAR misrepresents
the implicit Rohling 1983 Pfa equation; applying it to K-distributed,
Weibull, or log-normal clutter under-counts the observed Pfa by 1–3 orders
of magnitude. Closing this is correctness gate C12 partial — full closure
requires Lane G_b's empirical-Pfa calibrator.

## Files Changed

- NEW: `crates/echoforge-radar/src/detectors/cfar_alpha.rs` (893 LOC)
  - Closed-form `ca_cfar_scale_gaussian(N, Pfa)` (Skolnik §7.7.2).
  - Closed-form `os_cfar_scale_gaussian(N, rank, Pfa)` — Rohling 1983
    implicit equation
    `Pfa = ∏_{i=0}^{k-1} (N-i) / (N-i+α)` solved by bisection on α in
    `[1e-3, 1e6]`, ≤100 iters.
  - `calibrate_alpha_monte_carlo(variant, distribution, N, guard, Pfa,
    trials, seed)` — empirical-quantile estimator with deterministic
    SplitMix64 RNG. Supports CA / OS / GO / SO and Gaussian / Rayleigh /
    Weibull / KDistribution / LogNormal.
  - `alpha_library_lookup(...) -> Option<f32>` — exact-match lookup into a
    12-row const `ALPHA_LIBRARY` of Monte-Carlo-calibrated values for the
    canonical Wave 1 cruise configurations.
  - `resolve_alpha(...)` — dispatcher: closed form → library → MC fallback
    (100k trials, fixed seed `0x0CFA_A001`).
  - Local power-domain samplers (Weibull amplitude squared, K-distribution
    via Marsaglia–Tsang gamma + Rayleigh-power speckle, log-normal power).
  - 12 unit tests (see Validation).

## Strict-open posture

No measured-truth claims. ALPHA_LIBRARY values derived from Monte-Carlo
calibration on simulated K-distribution and Weibull amplitude
distributions cited to the standard radar handbooks above. The Gaussian
closed forms are exact textbook formulas, not measurements.
