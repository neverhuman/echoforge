## Notes
- Generated artifacts are under `outputs/demo/iranian-takeoff-v1` and are intentionally kept out of Git.
- Public text in generated artifacts uses public-proxy, statistical noise proxy, not measured truth, and not proprietary-equivalent language.
- The config library now covers multiple object classes beyond the Iranian takeoff demo, including commercial quadrotors, birds/flocks, balloons/kites/debris, ground vehicles, and infrastructure glints.
- Contested-airspace fields model robustness stressors such as traffic density, cochannel emitters, multipath severity, sensor dropout, and label ambiguity. They are not tactics or evasion optimization.
- The radar simulation remains an MVP proxy chain, not a measured-fidelity or operational sensor-performance model.
