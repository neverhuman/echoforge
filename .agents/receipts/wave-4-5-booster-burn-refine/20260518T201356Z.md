## 1. Motivation

Independent OpenRouter expert critique of the Wave 4 Lane H Tier 1 BOOST
detector flagged that the pre-H5 baseline modeled the rocket-assisted
launch transient as a single constant-acceleration band (5–20 m/s² ≈
1–2g) with no internal structure. Real solid-rocket-motor (SRM) thrust
profiles have three physically-distinct sub-phases that show up
differently in radar: a ~1–3 s high-thrust boost burn with a
characteristic smoothstep rise to peak then plateau; a brief
separation transient with a deceleration spike as the spent booster
physically decouples from the airframe; and a much-lower-acceleration
sustain phase under piston-engine thrust (~0.5 m/s²).

The pre-H5 baseline collapsed all three under "Tier 1 BOOST" and
hid model error. The H5 refinement makes the sub-phases first-class
classification outputs of the detector.

## 2. Model structure

Three new public items in
`crates/echoforge-radar/src/detectors/phase_tiered/tier_boost.rs`:

### 2.1 `BoostThrustProfile`

Solid-rocket-motor thrust curve model with four parameters and two
methods:

| Field | Default (Shahed-class) | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| `peak_acceleration_mps2` | 18.0 (~1.8g) | m/s² |
| `burn_duration_s` | 1.5 | s |
| `separation_transient_s` | 0.2 | s |
| `sustain_acceleration_mps2` | 0.5 | m/s² |

Methods:

- `acceleration_at(t_s) -> f64`: piecewise model implementing a
  smoothstep ramp (0–30 % of burn) → peak plateau (30–100 % of burn)
  → linear drop with −1 m/s² deceleration spike (separation
  transient) → constant sustain (piston engine).
- `velocity_at(t_s, rail_exit_velocity_mps) -> f64`: numerical
  integration of `acceleration_at` over 10 ms steps (analytic form
  would require splitting at the burn/separation boundaries and
  smoothstep is not a clean integral).
- `shahed_class_default()` constructor populated from the dossier
  envelope; also wired as `impl Default`.

### 2.2 `BoostSubState`

Discrete enum with three variants emitted per CPI:

- `BoostBurn` — during the high-thrust SRM burn.
- `SeparationTransient` — booster decoupling event.
- `PostSeparationSustain` — piston-engine phase.

### 2.3 `classify_boost_sub_state(accel_mps2) -> BoostSubState`

Pure-function classifier. Bands:

| Range (m/s², signed) | Sub-state |
|---|---|
| `[10, 30]` | `BoostBurn` |
| `[0, 1)` | `PostSeparationSustain` |
| `[-2, 5)` (else above) | `SeparationTransient` |
| other | `PostSeparationSustain` |

### 2.4 `BoostDecision` extensions

Added two fields to the existing per-CPI decision struct so every
return path (sub-horizon, marginal-LOS, gate-reject, M-of-N-fail,
detect) carries the sub-state classification and the signed
finite-difference acceleration used to make it:

- `sub_state: BoostSubState`
- `instantaneous_acceleration_mps2: f64`

### 2.5 `BoostTierDetector::evaluate` update

Computes the signed finite-difference accel via
`obs.current_acceleration_mps2()` at the top of `evaluate`, populates
`out.instantaneous_acceleration_mps2`, and runs
`classify_boost_sub_state` to populate `out.sub_state` before any of
the existing horizon / gate / M-of-N branches. All 4 pre-existing
tier_boost tests pass unchanged.

## 3. Parameter ranges and citations

All parameters are **public-proxy**, not measured-truth. Sources:

| Parameter | Range | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Boost burn duration | 1–3 s (model uses 1.5 s) | Dossier §5 "Booster burn time" + Sutton/Biblarz typical small RATO |
| Peak boost acceleration | ~1–3g (5–30 m/s²); model 18 m/s² | Dossier §5 boost-out kinematics + Sutton/Biblarz fig. 12-7 progressive-grain SRM |
| Rail-exit velocity | ~9 m/s (≈ 2·L_rail / t_boost) | Dossier §5 closed-form derivation |
| Release velocity | 25–35 m/s | Dossier §5 "Exit velocity (booster-augmented)" |
| Separation transient | ~0.2 s; −1 m/s² spike | Sutton/Biblarz §12-7 SRM tail-off + dossier description of decoupling event |
| Piston sustain accel | ~0.5 m/s² | Dossier §5 "powered cruise within ~10–30 s" timing implies ~0.5 m/s² average post-boost build-up |

**Citations:**

- Wave-A dossier
  `object-packs/public-proxy-v1/physics_dossier.md` §5 *Launch mode*
  ("Booster burn time 1–2 s", "Exit velocity 25–35 m/s",
  "Time from launch to powered cruise 10–30 s") — CSIS 2022, OSMP
  2023, Janes UAS 2023, ArmyRecognition 2022 lineage.
- Sutton, G. P. & Biblarz, O., *Rocket Propulsion Elements*, 9th
  ed., Wiley 2017, ch. 12 *Solid Propellant Rocket Fundamentals*,
  esp. fig. 12-7 *Typical thrust-time curve of a progressive-grain
  SRM*.
- RUSI Special Report 2022 *Iranian Loitering Munitions in
  Ukraine* + CSIS *Iranian One-Way-Attack Drones* (open-source
  reporting on Shahed-class launch mode).

## 4. Test counts

### 4.1 New tests in `tier_boost.rs` (3, all passing)

1. `boost_thrust_profile_shape_matches_smoothstep` — verifies
   `acceleration_at(0.0)=0`, `acceleration_at(burn*0.3)=peak`,
   `acceleration_at(burn-ε)=peak`, separation transient
   `acceleration_at(burn+0.1)=peak*0.15−1 ≈ 1.70 m/s²`, sustain
   `acceleration_at(burn+transient+1.0)=0.5`, and negative-time
   guard.
2. `boost_velocity_integration_reaches_release` — integrates from
   `rail_exit_mps=9.0` over `burn_duration_s=1.5` and asserts
   resulting velocity is inside the dossier envelope [25, 35] m/s
   and within ±5 m/s of the 30 m/s center.
3. `boost_sub_state_classification` — three direct classifier
   checks (20→BoostBurn, −1→SeparationTransient, 0.5→
   PostSeparationSustain) plus three end-to-end detector checks
   confirming `evaluate` publishes both the signed accel and the
   matching sub-state.

### 4.2 New gate in `tests/physics_correctness.rs` (1, passing)

`w45_h5_booster_burn_profile_reaches_release_velocity` — gate

velocity inside [25,35] m/s starting from 9 m/s rail-exit, post-

## 7. Strict-open posture

The booster-burn thrust profile and its sub-state classifier are
**public-proxy** expected models for a Shahed-class
solid-rocket-assisted launch transient. They:

- Cite the Wave-A
  `shahed-public-proxy-flight-envelope-v2` physics dossier
  (`object-packs/public-proxy-v1/physics_dossier.md` §5).
- Cite Sutton & Biblarz, *Rocket Propulsion Elements*, 9th ed.,
  ch. 12, esp. fig. 12-7 (typical progressive-grain SRM thrust-time
  curve).
- Cite RUSI 2022 / CSIS 2022 open-source reporting on Shahed-class
  launch mode.
- Do **NOT** claim platform-specific signature truth.
- Do **NOT** assert any measured-truth peak thrust, burn duration,
  or motor-specific impulse for any specific airframe.
- Encode a public-proxy dossier envelope (range [5, 30] m/s² peak,
  [1, 3] s burn, [25, 35] m/s release velocity) as the gate
  acceptance bounds, with the model's defaults sitting comfortably
  inside.

Per the dossier, every parameter here is a "bounded statistical
prior" not a "calibrated measurement of any specific airframe."
