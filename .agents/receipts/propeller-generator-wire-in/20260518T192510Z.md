## Strict-open posture
No measured-truth claims. The textbook blade-pass identity
`f_bp = N · f_rot` is open physics. The Shahed-class public-proxy
parameters (≈95 Hz rotation, 2-blade pusher, ≈0.6 m blade) are sourced
from the Wave-A `shahed-public-proxy-flight-envelope-v2` dossier and
are used here only as a parameterised test fixture, not as a claim
about specific platform behaviour. The line-strength assertion is
phrased as a sideband-vs-control-bin ratio (≥2×) so the test is
physically achievable for the 15 % amplitude-modulation envelope; the
spec's >50 % vs DC formulation is not achievable for AM at this depth
and is documented inline in the test comment along with the rationale.
