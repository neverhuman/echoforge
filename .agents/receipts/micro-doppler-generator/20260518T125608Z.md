## Notes
- **Model choice**: each rotor returns the *dominant* blade's signed velocity (`max_k |v_k(t)|`) rather than the algebraic sum across blades. The algebraic sum collapses identically to zero for any rotor with `N≥2` evenly-spaced blades (a standard identity for sums of equally-spaced unit phasors), which would have made every multi-blade rotor silent. Dominant-blade selection preserves the physically observable "blade-flash" character at the blade-pass frequency `N·f_rot`, which is precisely the spectral line that `MicroDopplerDetector` is built to lock onto. Documented in the struct doc comments.
